## Verdict

At the actual 82px card size, **25 of 26 cards are sufficiently recognizable and distinguishable for this bounded picture-art review**. They use a cohesive bright, dimensional, child-friendly illustration style, with clear silhouettes, contained subjects and enough detail at the rendered size. This supports the art direction for the reviewed Growing sheet; it is not runtime, lexicon, audio or full-game acceptance.

One card remains an editorial blocker: **crust**. Its picture reads first as a complete slice (in fact, overlapping slices) of bread. The browned perimeter is visible, but the image does not clearly make the outside crust itself the named target. A child hearing “crust” could reasonably identify “bread” instead. The authored definition is “Crust is the firm outside edge of bread,” so the picture and teaching point are not yet aligned strongly enough.

## Coverage

Reviewed all 26 contact-sheet subjects at 82px: duck, dock, rock, sock, lock, bell, hill, stamp, clamp, truck, brick, crab, flag, frog, drum, clock, plant, sack, tent, belt, pond, nest, raft, plank, crust and stump. This includes the 15 entries in the new-art provenance and the retained shared assets. No other subject was materially ambiguous at this size; close-looking pairs such as duck/dock, rock/lock and raft/plank remain visually separable. The dock’s boat/water context and raft’s water context help distinguish those words. The sheet’s glossy dimensional rendering is consistent enough across the mix of animals, tools and objects.

## Requested narrow correction and acceptance

Replace only the crust image with a simple, immediately readable bread corner or strip where the browned outside edge is the salient object, while retaining enough bread context to identify it as crust. At 82px, a first-time viewer should name the edge/crust rather than the whole slice. Keep the card isolated and visually consistent with the other set; do not add text or a label as a substitute for the picture. Re-render the same contact-sheet cell and review it at 82px before accepting this Growing art gate.

The other 25 reviewed subjects do not need regeneration based on this inspection. This review does not inspect gameplay rendering, picture pools, target hit areas, source-word eligibility, packaged recordings or listening quality. No source assets were changed, and no browser or provider was used.
