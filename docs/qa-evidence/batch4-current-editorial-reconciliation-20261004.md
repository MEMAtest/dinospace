## Per-game dimension reasons and recommendations

### Addition Adventure

- **Age-6 teaching — 4.5.** The sequence teaches joining visible groups, then missing-part number bonds, then familiar short stories. The whole/part display and count-on clue keep one strategy in focus, and zero is labelled as an empty group. The full matrix exercises the rendered tasks and explanation; the data/source review verifies bounded totals. Recommendation: retain the distinct whole/part model and keep story contexts short and familiar.
- **Meaningful progression — 4.5.** Put Groups Together, Number Bonds, and Addition Stories change the model and reasoning goal across six-question chapters rather than only enlarging operands. All chapters, unlocks, and full same-band replay were exercised at both widths. Replay star accounting was tested in the final matrix; its explicit 2★→3★ positive improvement is documented for Subtraction, not Addition. Recommendation: preserve this model progression and six-question held-answer structure.
- **Correctness and fair variation — 4.5.** Generator validation bounds chapter totals, checks canonical question membership, and requires unique in-range answer options; the queue is seeded/frozen for a run and uses recent-question avoidance where the finite pool allows. The full visual matrix completed every round and story replay at both widths with visible group counts agreeing with the equations. Recommendation: keep the generator and per-run freeze; preserve the specific 0-group case.
- **Reliability/navigation/persistence — 4.5.** The 5227 run and 5383 integration delta show completed-only chapter badge credit, reload persistence, Amari/Askia shelf separation, wrong/clue/correct logging and parent-world return. Recommendation: preserve the child/game-scoped collection and safe diagnostic boundaries. Evidence is local, not canonical production.

### Subtraction Station

- **Age-6 teaching — 4.5.** Take Away teaches removal, Compare Groups teaches pairing and unmatched difference, and Subtraction Stories transfers the ideas into familiar contexts. Equality and zero are valid; the corrected zero-removal clue no longer asks the child to count marked objects when none were removed. The independent 5386 check additionally confirms the visible zero tray and readable group labels. Recommendation: keep comparison language separate from take-away language and retain the explicit empty-state labels.
- **Meaningful progression — 4.5.** Take Away → Compare Groups → Subtraction Stories changes the reasoning model, not just the range. All 18 rounds per viewport and a full Take Away replay were completed in the retained final matrix; badges/replay were visible. Recommendation: retain the three distinct chapter models.
- **Correctness and fair variation — 4.5.** Source generation restricts subtraction to nonnegative results, validates question identity/model fields, bounds unique options, and freezes six unique questions at Start. The final matrix exercised equality (`5−5=0`), removing zero, and replay scoring: a 2★→3★ best added one star and an equal-best replay added none. The later ordinary 9−9 held result independently shows `0 (empty)` without answer leakage before selection. Recommendation: keep these zero/equality cases and the one-credit improvement rule.
- **Reliability/navigation/persistence — 4.5.** The baseline covers chapter completion/replay, profile separation, reload and duplicate-credit prevention; 5383 verifies the current integrated shelf and Maths parent route. The 5385/5386 zero-state evidence narrows the post-baseline visual behavior without broad re-run. Recommendation: keep this scoped lineage when the later audio bundle is integrated. No production behavior has been tested.

### Time Teller

- **Age-6 teaching — 4.5.** Clock Explorers introduces o'clock/half past and names the short red hour hand and long blue minute hand; Past and To adds quarter past/quarter to; Daily Routines gives a time-of-day context for familiar activities and includes hand-setting. The explanation avoids expecting an analogue clock alone to show morning/evening. Recommendation: keep routine context explicit and retain the continuous hour-hand movement explanation.
- **Meaningful progression — 4.5.** The three six-mission chapters move from reading familiar times, to quarter-hour language, to reading/setting times in routines. Full chapter runs and Daily Routines replays were completed at both widths. Recommendation: preserve the transition from read to set and context.
- **Correctness and fair variation — 4.5.** The generator reserves read/set tasks where needed, uses unique options, and property tests all 48 allowed hour/minute pairs and multiple seeded runs. The earlier 5221 wrap defect was corrected and the final 5227 UI run tested `12:45 + 15 → 1:00` and `1:00 − 15 → 12:45` on both widths. Recommendation: retain the tested rollover and unique distractor checks.
- **Reliability/navigation/persistence — 4.5.** The full matrix covers reload and ordinary Maths return; the 5383 route delta exercises Curriculum Quest → Time Detectives → clock, Keep playing, reload in the lesson and confirmed return to the selected Time Detectives module at desktop and mobile. Recommendation: preserve origin in history and the visible confirmed-return step. It remains local-only evidence.

### Number Line Jump

- **Age-6 teaching — 4.5.** Forward/back hops make each step child-controlled; Missing Numbers varies start, hop count or landing; Compare Values and Distances explicitly distinguishes a larger endpoint from a farther journey. One-step clues and explanations model how to count. The formerly text-only frog was corrected before the final 5227 matrix. Recommendation: keep the separate “larger number” and “farther” wording.
- **Meaningful progression — 4.5.** Forward and Back (0–10), Missing Numbers (0–20), and Compare Values and Distances (0–20) add new reasoning tasks across three six-mission worlds. All three worlds and a full Forward and Back replay were completed at both widths. Recommendation: retain the distinct task types rather than only increasing the number line ceiling.
- **Correctness and fair variation — 4.5.** The source validates equation, hop trail and landing together, rejects out-of-bounds hops, and distinguishes endpoint comparisons from distance comparisons. The rendered matrix exercised missing start/hop/landing forms, equal endpoints (“Same”), single-hop feedback and replay. The fe5 copy delta fixed singular `1 space`; source/corpus verification covers the replacement, while the narrow 5384 UI run did not naturally expose that compare sentence. Recommendation: retain both structural validation and singular/plural morphology.
- **Reliability/navigation/persistence — 4.5.** The final matrix shows child-scoped badge persistence, reload, sibling isolation, replay and no duplicate award; 5383 provides a representative current-route/collection integration delta. The older `math` celebration/diagnostic misclassification was fixed before the full final run. Recommendation: preserve the `numberline` identity through metrics, shelf and return handling. No canonical production run is established.
