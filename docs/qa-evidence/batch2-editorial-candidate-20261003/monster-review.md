# Monster Math — independent editorial review

**Scope:** review of the published five-dimension roadmap, retained 24-run baseline, identified production repair deltas, and the bounded packaged-runtime candidate evidence at `5203` (source `ead5a1d60d2ad5e2cacefc4cbcecb0aee7cec630`, JS `index-DZuRTFaZ.js`, SHA-256 `d63a6423477fd3cda6d2e9f66d15eefafc6a693e6039865a79b1f59cc534d85c`). No gameplay was rerun for this review and no source was changed.

The roadmap weights age-6 teaching, meaningful progression, correctness/fair variation, feedback/audio/visual usability, and reliability/navigation/persistence equally. Monster Math’s historical baseline is **3/5**. The prior local editorial review of the 5283–5285 lineage was **4.0/5**. Both remain tied to their own evidence identities.

## Provisional editorial view

These estimates are **provisional and unaccepted**. They synthesize the retained full baseline and the narrow follow-up deltas; they do not require replaying all 24 baseline rows after each repair. They are not a 4.5 certification or a production verdict.

| Equal-weight dimension | Provisional estimate | Editorial judgment and evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Age-6 teaching | 3.5/5 | The three episodes move from counting visible sets, to addition/subtraction with counters, to short word problems. Prompts are concrete; clue and retry language is gentle; a correct answer holds an explanation until Next. At 6b, the Story subtraction pre-answer screen showed the full number range, start point, and direction/count instruction without revealing the landing answer; the earlier d312 concern that range omitted the destination was fixed. A possible enhancement—not a number-line correctness defect—is letting children actively count backward or reveal one hop at a time before choosing, without exposing the answer. A new 5204 Starter pre-answer accessibility snapshot found a separate answer leak: the prompt asks the child to count oranges, while the picture group is announced as “10 counters 🍊” before selection. The ten individual picture nodes are hidden from the accessibility tree, so this label gives screen-reader users the answer. See [the mobile follow-up](../monster-mobile-next-20261003/report.md). |
| Meaningful progression | 4.5/5 | The retained baseline completed all six questions in all three episodes at both desktop and mobile, with episode rewards and next-episode access. The production supplement also checked ordinary-UI mobile replay with a new seed and different first prompt. This is meaningful quantity → operation → story progression with replay, not merely a number-range increase. These baseline rows remain valid; the 5203 runtime pass was a narrow narration delta and did not need to repeat the band matrix. |
| Correctness and fair variation | 4.5/5 | Full baseline play showed sampled visual models/equations agreeing with answers. The previously missing first-addend-over-10 case was later observed on production in the 9cc supplement (`18 + 2 = 20`), and the accessible-model repair exercised desktop `17 + 2`, desktop subtraction `14 − 5`, and mobile addition `2 + 14` with neutral pre-answer labels and agreeing solved model/equation/one explanation. `test/batch2MonsterMath.test.mjs` supplements that UI evidence: for 80 seeds per episode it checks deterministic six-question runs, no within-run repeats, one correct option and bounded options; it checks 500-seed answer-position balance, eight-question recent-history avoidance, broad unique pools, and model invariants including the full number-line route. These tests support variation and model correctness but do not replace UI evidence or audio review. |
| Feedback, audio, and visual usability | 4.0/5 | The 5202 accessibility repair verifies one worked-feedback paragraph alongside the solved model/equation and held Next state for both operations across widths. The 5203 bounded runtime checks verify packaged prompt/replay/explanation requests and native `playing`/`ended` ordering, plus Keep playing, mute/re-enable/replay, and confirmed-Back stop behavior at both widths. A new 5204 mobile check confirms normal vertical scroll exposes a 175.95×56px enabled Next button within the 390×844 viewport; clicking it advances one Story question and clears the prior explanation. This establishes playback mechanics, not intelligibility, pronunciation, pacing, clip joins, or child suitability; human listening remains open. The concrete duplicate-explanation failure is retained under the 6b production identity; do not erase it based on a local candidate pass. |
| Reliability, navigation, and persistence | 4.0/5 | The retained runs cover band completion, rewards and ordinary next-episode flow; production evidence separately checked reload persistence/profile isolation and the star repair. The 5203 delta confirms narration cancellation on Back and no revival on the destination gesture at both widths, with ordinary-UI diagnostic exports. This synthesis inherits those baseline checks and adds identified repair deltas. Final production confirmation is still needed for the affected post-6b feedback repair and packaged narration behavior; a new complete baseline is not indicated by the roadmap for these narrow changes. |

**Disposition:** candidate quality is materially improved from the 3/5 historical baseline. The provisional estimates do not award 4.5 or acceptance. Native packaged playback and identified model/progression deltas have positive evidence; subjective listening and the affected final-production checks remain open.
