# Batch 5 pure-phoneme source screening — 2026-10-04

## Result and boundary

This is a finite source-page review for the 32 distinct sounds behind the 37 missing runtime paths. No audio was downloaded, modified, decoded, or auditioned. No files or runtime code were changed. This review does not assert that no suitable recording exists anywhere; it records that none of the examined public candidates can currently be accepted against the recording contract.

**Outcome: 0/32 sounds have an accepted asset.** Wikimedia Commons provides several explicitly licensed phonetic samples, but the pages either document carrier-vowel contexts or fail to establish the required British school-phonics realization, isolation, or both. A license alone cannot close those gaps. All 37 runtime paths remain missing.

## Sources excluded by rights or task fit

- UCLA Phonetics Lab Data says the audio belongs to many different people and permission for use outside the site cannot be given; it says educational use is understood to be permitted by original owners, while linking is permitted. That is not an explicit app redistribution grant. The page's blanket CC BY-SA statement applies to the images, not the audio. Exclude those recordings unless a rights-holder grants permission.
- UCLA's phonetics archive page offers noncommercial CC BY-NC-SA reuse, but the archive consists of word/utterance recordings and the license bars commercial use. It is not a clean match for standalone phonemes or an app whose distribution terms are not confirmed noncommercial. Do not use it to fill these paths.
- Oxford Audio BNC access terms restrict reuse to scientific study/publication by dictionary writers, educational material, and researchers; this is not an explicit grant to package recordings in this app. Exclude.
- Commercial phonics packs or model-generated pronunciations remain leads only until their terms grant app redistribution and the exact files have been audited. No requests, payments, or generation were made for this report.

Primary rights/access pages: [UCLA Phonetics Lab Data](https://phonetics.ucla.edu/), [UCLA Phonetics Lab Archive reuse instructions](https://archive.phonetics.ucla.edu/archive_instructions.htm), [UCLA Linguistic Voice Quality project license](https://www.phonetics.ucla.edu/voiceproject/voice.html), [Oxford Audio BNC](https://www.phon.ox.ac.uk/AudioBNC).

## Decision and next step

No listed candidate should be copied into `public/audio/phonemes/en/` on the strength of this screening. The clearest route remains a fluent British phonics reader recording the 32 targets with an explicit packaging/distribution release. For any Commons candidate reconsidered later, first document the phonetic match and accent, obtain a human listening decision, satisfy the file-page attribution/license terms, decode the delivered file, and test all 37 runtime URLs in the actual UI. Filename, IPA label, file duration, automated transcript, or successful decoding alone is not pronunciation acceptance.
