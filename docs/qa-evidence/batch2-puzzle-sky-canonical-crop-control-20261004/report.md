## Confirmed c478 entry defect

On the original canonical c478 build, the Sky map’s “Start this sky” control is below the 390×844 initial fold. A normal locator click scrolled to and activated the control, then left the active-game header partly above the viewport. The repeated measurements were `scrollY=26` with header y=-18 after entering Growing/Challenge, and `scrollY=62` with header y=-54 on repeated Starter transitions; the 48px Back and sound controls were correspondingly clipped. The app had no horizontal overflow (document and body widths remained 390px). A normal upward scroll restored `scrollY=0`, header y=8, and both controls to visible 48×48 targets. Evidence: [entry after Start](mobile/sky-entry-after-start.png) and [natural scroll recovery](mobile/sky-entry-scroll-recovery.png). This is a genuine c478 issue; a follow-up report separately records verification of root’s immutable 5357 repair candidate.
