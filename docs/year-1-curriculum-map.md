# Year 1 Curriculum Quest map

This note records the boundary between the app's learning design and the sources it is informed by. It is not a claim that the app replaces classroom teaching or a school's planned sequence.

## School and scheme verification

The researched school is **Stewart Fleming Primary School, Witham Road, Anerley, London SE20 7YB (URN 137035)**, part of The Pioneer Academy. The school's Year 1 curriculum meeting identifies:

- Little Wandle for reading/phonics from EYFS to Year 2, alongside CUSP reading in Year 1;
- CUSP for writing;
- White Rose Maths;
- IPC for science, history, geography, DT, art, computing and PSHE;
- Penpals handwriting, PE Pro, the Bexley RE scheme and Language Angels French.

The [Stewart Fleming Year 1 page](https://stewartfleming.bromley.sch.uk/year-1/) and [Year 1 curriculum meeting slides](https://stewartfleming.bromley.sch.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Curriculum-Meeting-Slides-Aut-1-Year-1.pdf) are the school-specific evidence. Little Wandle is also listed by the Department for Education as a validated systematic synthetic phonics programme. App activities use original prompts and do not claim school or programme affiliation.

## Current implementation boundary

The **Curriculum Quest** card inside Explore & Languages contains three compact modules: Continents & Oceans, Time Detectives and Nature Lab. Their expanded Starter, Growing and Challenge pools now close the documented geography, history and science scope gaps, but they remain a researched practice set rather than complete coverage of every Year 1 objective. Each module reports attempts into the existing learning-evidence model. Space and dinosaur games remain optional enrichment and must not be presented as Year 1 curriculum mastery.

These are confirmed school-planning links, not a claim that every game round reproduces the school's teaching sequence. Summer 2 is included from the canonical overview; term letters available at the time of this implementation covered Autumn 1 through Summer 1.

The canonical school overview describes science, history and geography as taught through the International Primary Curriculum (IPC). The game therefore uses connected enquiry, communication and international-mindedness prompts, but the module registry below is an app design layer, not an IPC accreditation or a complete unit plan.

## Statutory England KS1 requirements (separate from school sequence)

The Department for Education's geography programme expects pupils to interpret maps, diagrams and globes and to name and locate the world's seven continents and five oceans. The history programme expects awareness of the past, chronological frameworks, similarities/differences and use of stories and sources. The science programme expects observation, simple questions, identifying/classifying and gathering/recording evidence. These statutory statements are broader than any one school's IPC sequence and should not be presented as school-specific confirmation.

## Scope coverage and difficulty design

The current data catalogue uses the following deliberate progression. Starter rounds favour one clear observation or classification; Growing rounds add related examples and vocabulary; Challenge rounds require comparison, ordering, route-following, source limits, or prediction followed by evidence.

| Module | Starter emphasis | Growing emphasis | Challenge emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continents & Oceans | Recognise continents and one map-key idea | Add oceans, directions and UK capital places | Add UK surrounding seas, globe/aerial/plan/digital-map concepts and careful weather/climate comparisons |
| Time Detectives | Choose a useful past clue | Sequence communication clues and personal timelines | Compare sources, check disagreements and distinguish one person’s memory from a wider claim |
| Nature Lab | Identify a plant, animal and season/material example | Cover animals and senses, plant structures, material properties, seasonal records and enquiry tools | Use fair-test reasoning and prediction→observation→conclusion investigations |

The app deliberately does not claim that a child has mastered the national curriculum or that Stewart Fleming taught any one round. A completion is an in-app practice event; evidence of classroom attainment remains with the school.

## Narration and offline boundary

Curriculum Quest uses reviewed, packaged ElevenLabs narration for its child-facing prompts, answer choices, feedback and key vocabulary. Tap-to-hear controls resolve only local clips in the offline manifest; there is no browser or device speech fallback. The module registry is the single source for the voice corpus, and the generator is resumable when the curriculum changes:

```sh
node scripts/generate-offline-voices.mjs --env=.env.local
```

The release gate requires the missing list to be empty. Reproduce that check from the registry with:

```sh
node --input-type=module -e "import {getMissingCurriculumVoiceAssets} from './src/data/curriculumVoice.js'; for (const {path,text} of getMissingCurriculumVoiceAssets()) console.log(path + '\\t' + text)"
```
