`Leak` uses three values: **Yes** means the clue states or nearly restates the answer; **Partial** means it strongly narrows the choice or repeats the prompt without much new evidence; **No** means it leaves a reasoning step. A counting, experiment, or comparison instruction is recorded as **No** when it requires the learner to do that step, even if the answer can then be derived.

## Source and evidence notes

The original source-gate report records an official NASA/JPL/ESA source for each mission fact and the runtime validator’s host allowlist. This audit checked that each mission’s linked official source is present and reviewed linked primary-source pages where wording raised a specific factual question. NASA JPL’s landing-technology description supports the larger-parachute / larger-payload relationship, and NASA describes radioisotope power systems as a type of “nuclear battery.” NASA’s rocket explainer confirms hot exhaust exits downward while the rocket moves upward. NASA Earth facts confirm liquid water covers most of Earth and that axial tilt causes seasons. The findings above are copy/teaching recommendations, not claims that the existing facts are broadly false.

Primary sources consulted:

- [NASA Science: Radioisotope Power Systems](https://science.nasa.gov/planetary-science/programs/radioisotope-power-systems/) — NASA calls RPS a type of nuclear battery and describes its electricity/heat role.
- [NASA JPL: NASA Tests Future Mars Landing Technology](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-tests-future-mars-landing-technology/) — larger parachutes are part of technology intended to enable larger payloads and must be developed/tested.
- [NASA Space Place: How Do We Launch Things Into Space?](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/launching-into-space/en/) — exhaust pushed down produces the opposite/upward rocket motion.
- [NASA Science: Facts About Earth](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/) — most of Earth is covered in liquid water; Earth’s axial tilt causes the yearly seasons.
- The per-mission NASA, NASA JPL, or NASA Ames links on each authored mission record are the authoritative fact references for the remaining entries; see [the source-gate inventory](batch6-source-gate-report-20261003.md).
